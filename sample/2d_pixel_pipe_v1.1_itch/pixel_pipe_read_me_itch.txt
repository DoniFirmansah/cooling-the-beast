Format
------
All in png spritesheet format and sharing one 4-colour gameboy palette.
16 x 16 pixels for pipes and tiles.
Graphics are supplied as sprite (transparency) and tile (non-transparent) spritesheets.
Aseprite format files for the above.


Notes
-----
All elements are exported as spritesheets to be more consise.
Individual frames can be exported from Aseprite, or you can contact me and I will add them as needed.

Liquid flows in both directions (a/b) and all directions (d1/d2/d3/d4) are catered for.
With some flipping you can achieve the same results with fewer sprites.
